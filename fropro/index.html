The Opportunity

Dormancy Is a
Timing Problem,
Not a Brand Problem.

FroPro has 9,670 shoppers enrolled in their Aisle program. 7,605 of them never claimed a cashback. The program went quiet — zero redemptions in the last 90 days, two signups. The shopper base didn’t disappear. They just haven’t had a reason to act.

The new flavor launch is that reason. Here’s the 5-part plan to convert dormancy into velocity.

The 5-Part Launch Plan

Priority Move

Initiative 02

📲

Re-engagement
Activation

Re-engagement is configured in Aisle but copy isn’t approved yet — which means 7,605 non-redeemers are sitting untouched. One SMS to this dormant base when the new flavor hits shelves = instant, measurable redemption volume. This fires Week 1.

Highest Leverage

7,605

Shoppers enrolled but never redeemed — ready to activate with one message

Initiative 01

🏪

WFM Launch Campaign

Dedicated cashback offer at Whole Foods for the new flavor. Repeat the “mission: clear WFM shelves” playbook that drove 1,945 activations. This is the proven lever — run it first, run it hard.

Weeks 1–4

Initiative 03

📍

Aisle Store
Locator

FroPro sends shoppers to their own store finder, but doesn’t have an Aisle-hosted Store Locator. Adding one means WFM discovery happens inside the Aisle ecosystem — trackable, attributable, and tied directly to the cashback offer.

Close the Loop

Initiative 04

🔍

Aisle Offers Page
Feature

FroPro isn’t on the Aisle Offers Page. Getting the new flavor featured captures discovery traffic — shoppers already browsing for deals who haven’t found FroPro yet. Zero lift required to get there.

New Discovery

Initiative 05

🔄

Loyalty Ladder

A post-redemption offer turns a first-time new-flavor buyer into a repeat customer. The 21% redemption rate proves the base responds when prompted. A two-step ladder for the new SKU builds long-term purchase habit.

Build Repeat

Why Now

The Window
Is 30 Days.

  • New flavor launches are a 30-day decision window. WFM buyers decide in the first visit or two. Miss the moment and the SKU gets marked as underperformer before the shopper base even knows it exists.

  • 7,605 FroPro shoppers haven’t activated yet — they’re enrolled, they know the brand, and they’re waiting for a reason. A new flavor with a cashback is that reason.

  • Three Aisle products are untapped. Re-engagement, an Aisle Store Locator, and the Offers Page are gaps that can be closed in days — each one directly accelerates the launch.
