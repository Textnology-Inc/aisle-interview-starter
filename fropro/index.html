The Plan

4 Initiatives.
Launch Day Through Week 4.

Initiative 01
WFM Launch Campaign
A targeted cashback offer for WFM shoppers: buy X of the new flavor, get Y back. Reduces purchase risk and drives trial velocity from day one.
Weeks 1–4 · Trial Driver
Initiative 02
Loyalty Ladder
After the first redemption, an automatic follow-up offer drives a second purchase. Converts a one-time trial buyer into a repeat customer.
Post-Redemption · Repeat Driver
Initiative 03
Aisle Offers Page
Feature the new flavor on Aisle's offers page. Captures discovery traffic from Aisle shoppers already browsing for deals — high-intent, low-cost reach.
Week 1+ · Discovery
Initiative 04
Store Locator
A WFM-specific store locator so shoppers can find the new flavor near them. Removes friction between intent and purchase.
Launch Day · Retail Activation
